SUMMONS PREPARATION SHEET
For Form 1 (J141), Small Claims Court

THIS IS NOT A SUMMONS. A summons only exists once the clerk of the court
issues it — the clerk assigns the case number, sets the hearing date, and
signs it. This sheet is what you write onto the official form.

Take this, the official Form 1, a copy of your letter of demand, and your
proof of delivery to the clerk of the Small Claims Court for your area.

BEFORE YOU GO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Take with you:

  - The completed Form 1, plus one extra copy for each defendant
  - A copy of your letter of demand
  - Your proof that the demand was delivered — the registered post receipt,
    or the sworn affidavit (Form 5)
  - Photocopies of any documents you listed in point 5
  - Your ID

The clerk checks the summons, sets a hearing date, and issues it. After it
is issued you or the sheriff must serve it on the defendant. If you serve it
yourself, rule 12 requires you to hand a copy to the defendant in person and
then lodge the original and proof of service with the clerk.

The clerk assists members of the public free of charge. You do not need a
lawyer, and you may not be represented by one at the hearing.
