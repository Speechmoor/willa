# Form J993 — Letter of Demand i.t.o. s29(1), Small Claims Courts Act 61 of 1984

Source: Department of Justice and Constitutional Development
https://www.justice.gov.za/forms/scc/scc_J993-Form04.pdf
Retrieved 2026-08-12. Statutory public form.

This is the authoritative structure. Willa fills it; Willa does not redesign it.

---

## Header — FROM (the plaintiff)

- Name
- Surname
- Address
- E-mail
- Date

## Header — TO (the defendant)

- Name of person or entity
- Surname (if applicable)
- Address
- E-mail (where known)

## DEMAND

**Paragraph 1.**
> I, `{plaintiff full name}`, hereby claim the sum of R `{amount}` from you
> in respect of `{set out the facts upon which the claim is based, e.g. rent,
> contract, etc.}` that arose on or about `{date}`.

The form permits a non-monetary alternative in place of the sum claimed
("other, insert what you are claiming from the defendant").

**Paragraph 2.**
> You are required within 14 (fourteen) days from the date of receipt of this
> demand, to pay or to settle the amount of R `{amount}`.
>
> If you fail to comply with this demand within the 14 (fourteen) days, I will
> institute action against you in the Small Claims Court to obtain a judgment
> for my claim against you.

**Signature block**
> (Signature, mark or fingerprint here)

## NOTE (printed on the form, reproduce verbatim)

(a) This letter of demand must be sent by registered post or delivered
personally by you to the person you are sending the demand to.

(b) If you have delivered the letter personally then your attention is drawn to
rule 7(2) and (3) stated below:

> "(2) The plaintiff must prove by means of an affidavit or by a registered
> post receipt that the letter of demand referred to in section 29(1) of the
> Act has been delivered to the defendant.
>
> (3) The plaintiff may use Form 5 in Annexure 1 for the affidavit referred to
> in subrule (2)."

## Notes for implementers

The signature is wet-ink, a mark, or a fingerprint. The form explicitly
accommodates plaintiffs who do not write. Any digital signature feature must
not become the only path through the flow.
