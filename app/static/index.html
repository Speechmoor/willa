If they do not pay

Fourteen days have passed since they received your letter and they have not paid or settled. You can now go to the clerk of the court and ask for a summons. This page prepares what you will write on Form 1 when you get there.

What you leave here with

A preparation sheet — your details, their details, and the Particulars of Claim. That last part is the hard one: Form 1 requires it to be brief and in point form, with no detailed history of the matter, and it is where most self-prepared claims come unstuck.

This is not a summons. Only the clerk can issue one — they check your papers, set a hearing date, give the claim a case number and sign it. Willa cannot do any of that. Take this sheet, your letter of demand and your proof of delivery to the clerk, and they will issue the summons from it.
